Before the first conversation

A few things
you might wonder.

Do I need to know what to build? +

No. Bring the business problem, what you’re hoping for, and what seems to be getting in the way. Understanding the need comes before choosing the solution.

Can you work with our existing tools? +

Yes. We look at what already works before recommending a change. The right move may be a connection between existing tools, a focused improvement, or a new system.

How do people stay in control of AI? +

AI can assist with research, knowledge, and repeatable work. People remain responsible for privacy, context, quality, and the final decision.

How can I learn or contribute? +

Write to hello@arzware.net with a short introduction, what you’re learning, and the kind of work you would like to explore.
